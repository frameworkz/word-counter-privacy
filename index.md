# Word Counter – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

Word Counter is developed by ClearPath Tools.

### Data collection

Word Counter does not collect, store, transmit or share personal information or website content.

### How the extension works

- Text entered into the extension or selected on a webpage is processed locally in your browser.
- Word counts and other statistics are calculated on your device.
- The extension does not send text or results to external servers.
- The extension does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

### Permissions

The extension uses browser permissions only to provide its features, including accessing selected text, displaying results on webpages, adding a right-click menu option and copying results when requested.

### Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.

### Contact

For privacy questions, contact ClearPath Tools using the publisher contact email listed in the Chrome Web Store.
